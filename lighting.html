Emotional Effect of Lighting Colors
Color Example Image Emotional Effect
Blue/Cyan Often represents isolation, sadness, or coldness. It is frequently used for night time scenes, creating a sense of calm or.
Red Signifies high-intensity emotions, including aggression, danger, passion, rage, and love. It is often used to make the viewer feel uneasy or to highlight intense moments.
Green Suggests nature, sickness, fear, corruption, or a surreal, unsettling, or dangerous atmosphere.
Orange Used to express warmth, energy, and enthusiasm, often creating a sense of excitement or urgency.
Pink/Magenta Often used to evoke innocence, femininity, or a dreamy, romantic atmosphere.
